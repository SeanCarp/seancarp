Projects Showcase

6-Axis Robot Arm Senior Captone

I led a team of 5 engineers to program a 4-foot, 6-DOF UR5e for Aurora Flight Sciences to plug arbitrary 3D surfaces using STEP files. I was responsible for the communication bridge between the Windows environment and the UR robot controller. I developed a trajectory algorithm using potential fields and complex homogenous transformations in Python.

Mobile Robotics Beehive Construction

I successfully implemented PID controllers for high-precision map navigation in Python. I then developed a custom clock-based LiDAR segmentation algorithm for obstacle avoidance and random walk exploration. Calibrated sensor thresholds to adapt to varying environment conditions during competition settings, such as the sensitivity to walls.

Satellite Servicer Mission Design

I designed a mission to replenish 9 satellites across 2 constellations, calculating phasing maneuvers, plane changes, total propellant and time requirements. Evaluated multiple phasing maneuver strategies to identify the most fuel-efficient and practical mission parameters. In the project, I used MATLAB to simulate the servicer mission and calculated the propellant and time required. I then re-ran the simulation changing the ordering of satellites to save propellant and time.

Intro to Mechancial Design Project

I developed advanced proficient in SolidWorks by mastering complex 3D modeling techniques, including loft features, surfaces, sheet metal, and weldments. I gained practical experience in engineering documentation and analysis through GD&T application, multi-part assembly motion studies, and FEA simulation using SimulationXpress.

FEA Thermal Switch

I worked with 6 engineers to study FEA on circular bimetal thermal switches using ANSYS FEA, APDL & Discovery to determine the materials and shape of a thermal switch to fit within the stress, frequency, and temperature requirements. I tested the new thermal switch design using ANSYS Transient Thermal, Modal and Steady State analysis.

WVU BAJA Data Acquisition (DAQ)

Volunteered for the BAJA DAQ sub-team to collect sensor data on the off-road buggy. The system displays crucial information (RPM, brake pressure, etc.) to the driver via an LCD screen and is designed to alert the pit team of breakdowns. This involved hardware integration, 3D printed enclosures, and microcontroller programming. In the event of a failure in the vehicle the driver can press and hold the button, which sends an email message to the pit team that the driver needs help.

GARY (General Artificial Response Yapper)

An attempt at a more efficient AI assistant. Instead of relying on a single large LLM, I am splitting the logic to integrate my own scripts and APIs. It uses an NLP pipeline (intent classifier, NLU, NER) to interpret input, run local scripts, or save reminders. A final, style-focused transformer mimics the user's attitude. The entire system is designed to run on a small, portable computer (LeRengade) for a challenge in efficiency.

LAC (Lunar Autonomy Challenge)

As project lead for the AIWVU club, I worked on this NASA/Johns Hopkins APL competition to create an autonomous system for navigating simulated lunar terrain. This was my first large collaborative coding project, using ROS to interface between a Python simulator and C++ (ORB-SLAMV3). I also solved complex networking challenges, setting up VLANs and VcXsrv to allow multiple users to access the single simulator laptop. We were able to place 5th out 31 teams nationally! Check out our standings!

CarpCade Arcade Cabinet

A high school project building a custom arcade cabinet for my father. I designed it to his specific elbow and eye height, sketching it by hand, creating a 1:12 scale model, and then a 1:1 scale profile. I integrated a Raspberry Pi, old monitor, speakers, and arcade controls. This project taught me a lot about woodworking, design for manufacturing, and repairability.
